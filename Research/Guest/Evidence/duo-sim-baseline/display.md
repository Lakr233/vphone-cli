# Display stack baseline (iPhone Duo simulator)

Simulator `duo-baseline-display` (28D2B766-…), iPhone19,4, iOS 27.1 24A94425,
Xcode 27.1. Data: `display.json` (46085 records, 21 captures), transcripts in
`raw/display/states/<capture>-{backboardd,springboard,safari}.txt`, posture-driver
output in `raw/display/posture/` (screenshots downscaled to 480 px).

## How it was collected

- `probes/display-backboardd.lldb`, `probes/display-springboard.lldb`,
  `probes/display-safari.lldb` are generated by `src/display/gen-probe.zsh` from
  `src/display/src/{helpers.lldbfrag,*.body,footer.lldbfrag}`.
- Getters are enumerated, not listed by hand. For each object, the probe takes
  every declared property getter plus every zero-argument, non-void method of
  the class and its superclasses below NSObject (`class_copyPropertyList`,
  `class_copyMethodList`). It then drops names with side effects: `set*`,
  `add*`, `remove*`, `invalidate*`, `_lock_*`, `_queue_*`, `_workQueue_*`,
  `copy*`, `create*`, `start*`/`stop*`, focus and drag lazy getters, and others.
  The full lists are in `helpers.lldbfrag`. The probe calls each getter through
  a typed `objc_msgSend` cast chosen from the method's return type encoding. A
  getter that returns a block, a pointer, a union or an unknown struct gets a
  record with an error and is never called. UITraitCollection is the exception:
  32 display-relevant getters are named in the probe, because third-party
  categories add hundreds more.
- lldb expressions here cannot use `@try`. A getter that raises an exception
  aborts the whole expression, so `src/display/iterate.zsh` traced the last
  getter called to a file and added it to a skip list. Only one getter raised:
  `-[CAWindowServerDisplay secureIndicatorsEnabled]`. It is recorded with the
  error "raises an Objective-C exception in this process (not called)".
- Each capture: `posture.zsh` (swap or status), then the backboardd probe, then
  the SpringBoard probe (plus MobileSafari for 08-13). `src/display/capture.zsh`,
  `orient-cycle.zsh`, `merge.zsh`.

### Orientation: what took, and how

- iPhone SpringBoard on the cover is portrait-only, so the home screen gives no
  orientation data. Captures 08-13 put MobileSafari in front.
- The swap tool's `-deviceOrientation=<o>` option is what rotates apps. It
  drives SpringBoard's pose. It does **not** change backboardd's
  `BKOrientationManager.currentRawDeviceOrientation`, which stays 0 after boot.
- `BKSHIDServicesSetDeviceOrientationForAutomation` (BackBoardServices, called
  in SpringBoard) changes BK's raw orientation and SpringBoard's `UIDevice`
  orientation. While the swap tool's pose orientation was landscape, it did not
  rotate the app back. Captures made that way were discarded
  (`raw/display/states/discarded/`). It was last called with 1, so captures 08-10
  show raw orientation 1; after the reboot it is 0 again.
- Mapping that was observed (Safari = the app's own `UIDevice` and scene orientation):

| swap option | cover: Safari dev/io, scene bounds | inner: Safari dev/io, scene bounds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| portrait | 1/1, 466×678 | 1/**4**, 951×669 |
| landscapeLeft | 4/4, 678×466 | 3/**1**, 669×951 |
| landscapeRight | 3/3, 678×466 | 4/**2**, 669×951 |

On the inner display the interface orientation is a quarter turn from the
device orientation: holding the device in portrait gives landscape UI on the
inner panel. The `orientation` field in the records is the swap option name.

## History-dependent values

1. **The inner UIScreen before and after the first unfold.** At boot
   (01, and again 11 after a reboot):
   - bounds 669×951 and `_interfaceOrientation` 0 (`orientation: unknown (0)`)
   - `_peripheryInsets` t/b
   - traitCollection without size classes (0/0)

   From the first unfold on, in every posture including closed (02-07):
   - bounds 951×669 and `_interfaceOrientation` 4
   - `_peripheryInsets` l/r
   - horizontal/vertical size class Regular (2/2)

   Closed after open (03, 07) keeps the post-unfold values. The FBS current
   configuration seed goes 3 → 5 at the first unfold. The inner
   CAWindowServerDisplay is `landscapeLeft`, the CADisplay `rot90`, and BK
   holds orientation 4 for the inner UUID, already at boot.
2. **The cover UIScreen keeps the last orientation the cover had.**
   - After 08-closed-LL/LR it stays 678×466 with `_interfaceOrientation` 4/3
     while the inner is in use (09-book-LL/LR, 13-book-LL/LR). The `book`
     anchor swap to closed inherits the current device orientation, so the
     cover rotates first.
   - `12-open-LL/LR` (no anchor) keeps the cover at 466×678.
3. **The SpringBoard inner primary scene orientation with device portrait.**
   - It is 1 when the swap unfolded from closed: 09-book-P, 12-open-P, and
     02/04 on the home screen.
   - It is 4 when the swap started on the inner: 10-open-P, 13-book-P.
   - Safari is at io 4 in both cases, and
     `UIApplication.activeInterfaceOrientation` is 4.
4. **Display UUIDs change on every boot.** CADisplay uniqueId/UUID, FBS
   hardwareIdentifier, CAWindowServerDisplay systemIdentifiers and
   FramebufferIdentifier all change. Compare by name, displayId and identity,
   never by UUID.

Repeatability: closed 03 vs 07, open 02 vs 05 and book 04 vs 06 match except
for the volatile fields (touch-stream creation times).

## Digitizer → display mapping (backboardd)

From `_queue_perDisplayInfo` and `_queue_displayUUIDForDigitizerService:`:

| display | service | flags |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cover (LCD) | `CoreDevice touchscreen(<cover UUID>)`, senderID …03 | builtIn, authenticated, transport CoreDevice, `mainDisplayDigitizerService` |
| inner (LCD-1) | `CoreDevice touchscreen(<inner UUID>)`, senderID …04 | builtIn, authenticated |
| Resizable | `CoreDevice touchscreen(<Resizable UUID>)` …05 and an accessory 0xACEFADE… (eventSource 2, displayIntegrated, not authenticated) | |

Each `BKDirectTouchState._interfaceOrientation` follows its display's BK
orientation: the cover 1, the inner 4 (1 or 2 when the inner app is rotated).
There is no AOP or pencil digitizer, and no chamois display.

Incident: at 14:45:36 every CoreDevice HID service disappeared from backboardd
("*** lost main display digitizer ***", IOHIDEventSystem connections removed).
The host side caused it; this sim did nothing at that moment. It left
10-open-LL/LR without digitizers. Those two captures were discarded, the sim was
rebooted, and the open orientations were redone as 12-open-*.

## Missing, nil and errored

- **nil on both panels:**
  - CADisplay: productName, containerId, externalDisplayAttributes,
    allowed/preferred/supportedHDRModes, availablePresets, currentPreset,
    `_copyDebugProperties` (NULL)
  - CADisplayStateControl: `_copyAll{Power,FrameRate}AssertionInfo`
  - CAWindowServerDisplay: brightnessCapabilities, virtualDisplayMode,
    averagePixelConstraints, TVMode, cloneMaster, cursorDictionary
  - CAWindowServer: displayExternalSyncManager
  - FBS: `FBSDisplayIdentity.uniqueIdentifier`,
    `FBSDisplayConfiguration.uniqueIdentifier`, `originatingConfiguration`
  - UIScreen: mirroredScreen, `_overrideTraitCollection`, `__eventDisplay`,
    `_initialDisplayContext`, `_previousHardwareIdentifier`,
    focusGroupIdentifier
  - `+[UIScreen _carScreen]`, `UITraitCollection._systemDisplayIdentifier`,
    `UISDisplayContext.displayEdgeInfo` / `exclusionArea`
  - BK: mainDisplayAOP/PencilDigitizerService, chamoisDisplayUUID,
    `_frozenDisplayIDs`
- **Not called (type):**
  - CADisplay.timingsControl (`^v`)
  - CAWindowServerDisplay blend/displayColorSpace (`^{CGColorSpace}`),
    detachingDisplay, systemGamma and blackpointAdaptation (`CGColorTRC`
    union), the six callback blocks
  - UIScreen `_fallbackTraitCollection` (`^v`)
- **Raises:** `CAWindowServerDisplay.secureIndicatorsEnabled`.
- **Declared but not implemented:** UIScreen `_isEligibleForFocusInteraction`,
  `_isEligibleForFocusOcclusion`, `_linearFocusMovementSequences`,
  `_preferredFocusMovementStyle`, `traitOverrides` (protocol properties).
- **Absent APIs:**
  - CAWindowServerDisplay has no `contextIds` getter. The probe records
    `contextIdAtPosition:` at the panel centre instead; it is volatile.
  - There is no `CADisplay.maximumFramesPerSecond`, `colorGamut` or
    `overscan` getter. The equivalents are `refreshRate`,
    `currentMode.colorGamut` and `overscanAmount` / `overscanAdjustment`.
  - `extendedProperties` exists only on CAWindowServerDisplay, not on FBS.
  - `_UIScreenDisplayConfiguration` does not exist; it is
    `UIScreen.displayConfiguration` / `fbsDisplay`.
  - `SpringBoard._accessibilityFrontMostApplication` (a probe convenience) is
    not available.
